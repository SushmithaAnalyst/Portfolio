Logistics & Supply Chain · SQL + Tableau 01

DHL Group Q2 2026 Performance Analysis

DHL Supply Chain's Q2 2026 results caught my attention, particularly its 12.9% YoY revenue growth and momentum in the Americas. I used DHL Group's official Statbook data covering six quarters, structured it in SQLite, analyzed and validated the figures with SQL, and built an interactive Tableau dashboard to explore the broader performance story.

Key findings: DHL Express led divisional growth at 21.5%, eCommerce recorded 9.5% organic growth despite a 3.7% reported decline, Group EBIT grew 30.0% compared with 12.8% revenue growth, and Technology represented 22.3% of Supply Chain new-business wins versus 11.5% of current revenue.

Operations · Supply Chain 02

Warehouse Shipment Delay Analysis

54.8% of orders arrived late, and the easy explanation was warehouse execution. I pulled 180,519 orders across five markets and 38 months of order data, built an interactive dashboard to check the pattern by shipping mode, region, and time, and the data pointed somewhere else entirely.

The finding: Late rates stayed within 0.8 points of each other across all five markets (54.4% to 55.2%) and held flat for 38 straight months with no seasonal pattern. First Class alone ran 95.3% late, the mode promises 1-day delivery against a 2.0-day actual average. That kind of consistency does not come from warehouse execution. It points to an Order Management System committing delivery windows the fulfillment process was never able to hit.

Operations · Demand Optimization 05

The bikes were there. They just weren't where people needed them.

Availability isn't the problem : redistribution is. By analyzing demand at the station level, a clear picture emerged: weather barely matters, weekends barely shift demand, but 6 PM is where everything breaks down. Commuters flood stations that weren't stocked for them.

The fix: Station-level rebalancing timed to evening commuter peaks , not broad network averages , reduces shortages at high-demand nodes and idle inventory at low-demand ones at the same time.

Urban Strategy · Market Research 06

The Red Line is coming. Roseland isn't ready.

Transit access without economic access doesn't move communities forward. I used demographic, income, and food-access data to map exactly what Roseland was missing , and what the CTA extension created an opportunity to fix. The demand was clearly there. Content Analytics · Tableau 07

Views don't mean success. 537 videos prove it.

Thirteen years of YouTube data. 537 videos. Every platform metric you can name. The single strongest predictor of long-term performance isn't subscriber count, production budget, or total views , it's what happens in the first 60 days. That's the window most content teams aren't watching closely enough.

The counterintuitive finding: High-view videos consistently underperformed on engagement rate. Content that drove early interaction compounded into better long-term outcomes : a fundamentally different optimization target than most teams use.

Key finding: COVID-19 devastation, an $8.1B debt load, and a blocked $3.8B lifeline left no recovery path. Post-shutdown, fares on Spirit routes rose 23% as Frontier absorbed 100+ routes overnight.
